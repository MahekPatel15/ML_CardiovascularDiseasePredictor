Machine Learning Decision Pipeline

This dashboard is backed by a clinical Decision Tree model trained on standard cardio patient records.

Data Cleaning & Integrity

Originally consisting of 70,000 data rows, the dataset was sanitized to remove invalid clinical records (e.g. negative blood pressure or where systolic blood pressure was lower than diastolic) and height/weight outliers (extreme values outside 100-220cm and 30-200kg). The final model was trained on 65,435 high-quality patient vectors.

Standard Normalization

To prevent continuous values (such as age, height, and blood pressure) from dominating binary indicators (such as physical activity or smoking), feature scaling is executed. A StandardScaler was fitted on the training split to center the features around 0 mean and scale to unit variance.

Decision Tree Classifier

A supervised Decision Tree Classifier was fitted using Scikit-Learn. The tree depth was restricted to max_depth=6 to maintain simple decision boundaries, prevent overfitting, and ensure the model remains interpretable and robust to new clinical observations.

Feature Importance Mapping

The decision tree weights clinical indicators differently. Based on training analysis, blood pressure and age are the primary root classifiers, followed by cholesterol levels.
