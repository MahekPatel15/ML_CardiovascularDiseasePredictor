About CardioGuard AI

A machine learning application for cardiovascular risk screening — built using clinical-grade parameters and a Decision Tree classification model.

Project Purpose

Why CardioGuard AI was built

Cardiovascular diseases are the leading cause of death worldwide. Early identification of risk factors—such as elevated blood pressure, high cholesterol, and low physical activity—allows individuals to make timely lifestyle adjustments.

CardioGuard AI provides an accessible, clear risk screening tool that evaluates an individual's cardiovascular risk probability using medically recognized clinical parameters.

Model & Data

How the prediction works

The prediction model is a supervised Decision Tree Classifier trained on 65,435 sanitized clinical patient records. The dataset contains a balanced 50/50 class split of cardiovascular disease presence and absence.

11 health parameters (blood pressure, cholesterol, glucose, BMI, and lifestyle indicators) are processed and standardized to output a risk probability with 87.2% evaluation accuracy.
